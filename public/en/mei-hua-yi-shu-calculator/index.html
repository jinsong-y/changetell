CHANGE_TELL guide

Mei Hua Yi Shu Calculator

CHANGE_TELL is a deterministic Mei Hua Yi Shu and I Ching calculator. It calculates the hexagram first, then asks AI to explain the fixed result in readable language.

The important distinction is simple: AI does not choose the hexagram, moving line, body/use roles, five-element relation, or auspiciousness. Those values come from local rules.

What the app calculates

For each reading, CHANGE_TELL computes the original hexagram, mutual hexagram, changed hexagram, moving line, body trigram, use trigram, body/use relation, five-element status, and seasonal context. The result is then passed to Gemini as locked data for interpretation.

Time cast and number cast

Time Cast uses deterministic calendar values. Number Cast lets the user provide numbers for the upper trigram, lower trigram, and optional moving line. Both modes use the same principle: calculation first, explanation second.

Who it is for

CHANGE_TELL is for people who want an I Ching or Mei Hua Yi Shu reading without treating AI as the source of randomness. It is especially useful when a user wants bilingual explanation, transparent calculation, and a consistent structure for repeated study.
